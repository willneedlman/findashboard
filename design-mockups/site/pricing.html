Questions

Before you upgrade.

Where does the data come from?

Live market data via Tradier, fundamentals from FMP with a yfinance fallback, and macro series from FRED. Valuation models backstop missing inputs with Damodaran industry data.

Can I trade real money?

The terminal runs paper trading over Tradier today. It's the analytics and execution-practice layer — bring your own broker for live orders.

Is this investment advice?

No. Alphatape Terminal is a research and educational tool. Nothing in it is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — monthly, no contract. Downgrade to Starter and keep your watchlists and journal.
